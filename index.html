RULES & SCORING

  • Neon Pellet: Grants +10 points and extends your snake length.
  • Golden Star: Spawns occasionally for limited time. Grants +50 points!
  • Infinity Mode: Borders wrap around endlessly. Speed climbs over time.
  • Obstacle Mode: Eat 5 pellets to unlock the exit portal and reach the next labyrinth!
  • Time Attack: 60-second frenzy with high-value bonus pellets.
